10 Ways to Accessorize Your Guillotine Look This Season

Death has never been more fashionable! Here are our top tips:

  • Red Scarf: The ultimate statement piece. Blood-resistant fabric recommended.
  • White Dress: Classic, elegant, and hides absolutely nothing. Perfect for your final moment.
  • Short Hair: Less to grab, more practical. Plus it's very on-trend!
  • Minimal Jewelry: Keep it simple. Anything fancy might be confiscated anyway.
  • Comfortable Shoes: You'll be walking to the scaffold. Make it count.

From Corsets to Chaos: Parisian Style Tips

Before (Monarchy Era):

  • Powdered wigs (2 feet tall minimum)
  • Corsets (breathing optional)
  • 10 layers of silk
  • Excessive makeup

After (Revolution Era):

  • Natural hair (or short if guillotined)
  • Simple cotton dresses
  • Practical, comfortable clothing
  • Minimal makeup (peasant chic!)

How to Host a Revolutionary Dinner Without Losing Your Head

✅ DO:

  • Serve bread (lots of it)
  • Discuss equality for all
  • Toast to the Republic
  • Keep portions modest
  • Use simple pottery

❌ DON'T:

  • Serve cake (Marie Antoinette flashbacks)
  • Mention your noble ancestry
  • Use fine china
  • Serve champagne
  • Complain about bread prices

5 Tips for Simple Living in an Era of Violent Political Change

  1. Stay Neutral: Don't take sides. It's safer that way.
  2. Hoard Bread: You never know when the next shortage will hit.
  3. Keep Your Head Down: Literally. Avoid attention.
  4. Learn to Knit: Productive and calming while watching executions.
  5. Practice Your Revolutionary Songs: Knowing "La Marseillaise" might save your life.
